How a project runs

From raw footage to final export.

01

Brief & footage

You send over raw clips, brand references and the goal for the piece — a launch, an ad, a short-form series. I review everything before touching a timeline.

02

Structure & pacing

The story gets built first: what stays, what goes, and how it's paced — for retention if it's short-form, for clarity if it's a brand film.

03

Motion & sound

Custom animation, captions, transitions and sound design layered in — built frame by frame in After Effects, never dropped in from a template pack.

04

Color grade

A consistent, cinematic grade that keeps your brand's look intact without flattening the source footage.

05

Review & revisions

You get a private link to review. Revisions continue until the pacing and look are right — not until a round counter runs out.

06

Delivery

Final export in whatever format and aspect ratio you publish in, ready to upload — no watermarks, no extra steps on your end.

About

A one-person studio, run like a real one.

I'm Andrej, a freelance motion designer and video editor working out of Serbia. Every project is built in Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro, with an eye for restraint borrowed from the products I admire most — clean typography, deliberate motion, nothing that isn't earning its place on screen.

No templates, no stock transitions. Just a frame-by-frame approach to making brands look considered, whether that's a 16:9 brand film or a fast-paced 9:16 cut built for the scroll.
